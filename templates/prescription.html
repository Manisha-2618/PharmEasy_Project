What is a Valid Prescription?

Government regulations require a legally valid prescription containing the following 4 critical elements:
  • Doctor's Details: Name, Clinic/Hospital stamp, and Medical Registration Number.
  • Patient Information: Patient name, age, and consultation date.
  • Medication Specifics: Exact medicine name, strength, dosage schedule, and duration.
  • Doctor Signature: Physical or digital authorized medical signature.
